# Check-in

**Goal:** a short pulse. Most days nothing is due, and that is a good check-in.

## Before you open

Read the open action fields, the solution approaches at `agreed` or
`in-practice` with their success criteria, and what the last check-in agreed.
Sweep for three things, kept apart: whether the agreed approaches are holding;
anything structural they have not mentioned; a quick win you could fix today.

Open with a specific reason, not a bare "how are things?":
> "It's three weeks since we set up the proposal blocks — and your calendar looks
> busier than it did. How have things actually been?"

## The short path

Nothing due, nothing moved, nothing to fix, and a short untroubled answer → two or
three sentences and close. Don't fish. **Write nothing** except the log line.

## When there is something

1. **How it has been** — their answer first. Then add one observation, two at
   most, with its source, ending on a question. A quick win: just offer it — say,
   yes, do, report — and save it as a solution approach.
2. **Are the approaches holding?** Hold each one against its success criteria —
   look before you ask. For things you set up: did they *survive*, or get booked
   over or bypassed? For things they committed to: what happened, not what they
   intended. Something learned → a line in the approach's feedback; nothing new →
   nothing. Not holding is information: offer to rework it (`co-design`). Holding
   for good → `established`; ask whether the field is `resolved`.
3. **What is still open** — fields at `identified` that never got worked on:
   "We named this six weeks ago — still costing you, or sorted itself out?"
   Still hurts → co-design; gone → `dropped`; not now → leave it.
4. **Anything to think through?**

**Bigger than a check-in?** Ask, don't slide: "That's bigger than a check-in —
make it a co-design now, or park it?" Several new structural things → offer
`retro` or `first-look`. Parked → the field gets `stage: parked` and its description of the problem
says why it is waiting; a new topic becomes a new field holding only what they
said. The log records it. A topic parked for the third time
gets named as such.

Close by writing the check-in document and saying what the next one will look at.
