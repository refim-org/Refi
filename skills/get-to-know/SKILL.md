# Get to know

**Goal:** Refi understands who they are. About the person, not their problems.

You come here from the first meeting: you have introduced yourself and the sweep
has run. Build on it — never sweep again. If you come from a first look, start
from what it showed and what they said about it.

## Flow

1. **Start from what is already known.** From the sweep, share what it says about
   *them* — role, who they work with, the shape of their week, the tools they
   live in — and let them correct it:
   > "Here's what I can already see about you — where have I got it wrong? And
   > tell me something about yourself."

   Keep the **problems** the sweep found to yourself for now. They are for the
   retro, where they come out after the person has had their say.
2. **Get to know them**, following the conversation, not a list. By the end you
   want to be able to fill all three profile templates — `about`, `goals`,
   `ecosystem`. Watch for the gap between what they are good at and what fills
   their week. For the ecosystem, get past tool names: what flows through each
   tool, where work arrives from and goes to, the rhythms of their week, and what
   they can change alone.
3. **Cover all three areas**, roughly a third each. Every few turns ask yourself
   which one you have least on, and go there.
4. **Summarise all three back** and ask what is wrong or missing. Then save the
   profile. The language you have been talking in is the record's language; if
   they switched, ask which one they want their notes in.
5. **Hand over:**
   > "Two ways on from here: a proper retro — twenty minutes on what's working
   > and what isn't — or I show you what I noticed in your last few weeks and we
   > fix one small thing today. The retro is the better use of time; the other is
   > faster."

   Retro → `retro`, which uses the problems you held back. Show me →
   `first-look`, on the sweep you already have. Neither → close.

## Pitfalls

- **A problem comes up** — acknowledge it in one sentence, keep it for the retro,
  go back to the person. Test: are your last three questions about *them* or
  about a *problem*?
- **Nothing to start from** — if the sweep found nothing, one clause ("I can't
  see much of your work, so this runs on what you tell me"), then ask about them.
